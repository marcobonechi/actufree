## Full description (4000 max)

    Actufree is a small collection of puzzle games that asks nothing of you.
    No ads. No accounts. No sign-in. No analytics, no trackers, no third-party
    SDKs. The Android build does not even request the internet permission, so
    it cannot send anything anywhere.

    SUDOKU

    - Four difficulties, from puzzles that flow to ones that need a colouring
      chain.
    - Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and none of them require a guess.
    - Hints name the technique and explain the reasoning in a sentence, so a
      stuck board teaches you something instead of just filling itself in.
    - Pencil marks, mistake highlighting, and your game saved per difficulty.

    BLOCK BLAST

    - Drag shapes onto an 8x8 board and clear full rows and columns.
    - Clearing two lines at once is worth four times as much as clearing one.
    - Every piece you are dealt fits somewhere, and a board in trouble is
      dealt pieces that can dig it out.
    - Your run is saved as you play, and your best score is kept.

    PRIVACY

    Actufree collects nothing. There are no servers, so there is nothing for
    anyone to see. The app saves four things in its own private storage: your
    appearance preference, one unfinished Sudoku per difficulty, one unfinished
    Block Blast run, and your best Block Blast score. That is the whole list.
    None of it leaves your device, and uninstalling deletes all of it.
