Privacy Policy

Actufree · Effective 27 August 2026

Actufree collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device, because the app has no way to send it anywhere.

What is collected

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data of any kind. We have no servers, and there is no way for us to see anything about you or your use of the app.

What is stored on your device

Actufree saves a small amount of data in its own private storage:

  • Your appearance preference — device default, light, or dark.
  • One unfinished Sudoku puzzle per difficulty, so you can resume it.
  • One unfinished Block Blast run, so you can resume it.
  • Your best Block Blast score.

That is the whole list. It is stored in the app's private storage area, which is not readable by other apps, and it never leaves your device. Uninstalling Actufree deletes all of it.

Network access

The released Android app does not request the INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make network connections at all. The iOS app makes no network requests. This is not just a promise about what we choose to do; the app is not capable of it.

The browser version

Actufree can also be played in a browser, and that version is served by GitHub Pages. Serving any page means the host sees the request: GitHub receives your IP address and the usual web server log entries, exactly as it does for this page you are reading. That is GitHub's collection, not ours, and we neither see it nor receive it.

The app itself still sends nothing. Saved games live in the browser's own storage rather than in app storage, and clearing site data deletes them. The claim above about the Android build being unable to reach the network is a claim about the Android build; a page served over the web is necessarily fetched over the web.

Advertising and analytics

There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no attribution or measurement software, and no social media integration. No advertising identifier is read.

Permissions

Actufree requests no runtime permissions. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or anything else.

Children

Actufree is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children, and it displays no advertising.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Actufree ever changed what it stores or gained the ability to use the network, this policy would be updated before that version was released, and the effective date above would change.
